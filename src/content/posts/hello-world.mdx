This stub post is here to validate that MDX loading, frontmatter parsing, Zod validation, and the cross-reference to `src/data/resources.ts` all work end-to-end.

It will be deleted or replaced before Phase 02 merges back to `production`. The seed post in Plan 02-05 is the real launch content.

## Why a stub matters

Without at least one MDX file, `import.meta.glob` returns an empty record and `loadPosts()` returns `[]` — which makes every downstream plan (`/posts` list, `/posts/:slug` detail, recent-posts widget) impossible to verify visually. Three more tasks would be working blind.

## What this post is NOT

It is not editorial content. It is not search-indexable yet (no `<Helmet>` until Plan 02-05). It is not styled for production — the `prose` theme integration happens in Plan 02-03 when the detail page lands.
